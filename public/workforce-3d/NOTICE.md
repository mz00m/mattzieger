## Modifications

This fork adapts the original Tiny World Builder into "Workforce Busytown 3D" —
a 3D voxel-tile visualization of the U.S. public workforce investment system.
Modifications include:

- A custom workforce world preset placing entities for the federal, state, and
  local districts (DOL/ETA, AJC, community college, employers, etc.).
- Funding-flow vehicles tinted per WIOA title.
- Persona tours adapted from the existing 2D `/workforce` visualization.

The full modified source for this subdirectory is available in the
[mattzieger.com repository](https://github.com/mz00m/mattzieger) at
`public/workforce-3d/`.
